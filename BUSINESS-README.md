A comprehensive business framework for launching and scaling AI-assisted development services across multiple entrepreneurship verticals. This guide focuses on the business, legal, marketing, and sales aspects of building a successful AI development consultancy.

Transform the AI development services industry by creating a systematic, scalable approach to:

## 🏢 Business Structure & Legal Foundation

### Entity Formation
**Recommended Structure: LLC or Corporation**
- **LLC Benefits**: Pass-through taxation, operational flexibility, personal liability protection
- **Corporation Benefits**: Investment readiness, stock options, clear governance structure
- **Hybrid Approach**: Start as LLC, convert to C-Corp when seeking investment

### Legal Requirements Checklist
- [ ] Business name registration and trademark search
- [ ] Federal EIN (Employer Identification Number) acquisition
- [ ] State business license and registration
- [ ] Professional liability insurance
- [ ] Technology errors & omissions insurance
- [ ] Client contract templates and terms of service
- [ ] Privacy policy and data handling compliance (GDPR, CCPA)
- [ ] Intellectual property assignment agreements

### Compliance Framework
- **Data Protection**: GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2 compliance planning
- **AI Ethics**: Responsible AI development guidelines
- **Client Confidentiality**: NDA templates and data security protocols
- **Industry Standards**: ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II preparation
